If money or information already moved

01 · STOP

End contact

Do not send another payment, share another code, or install requested software.

02 · CALL

Contact the institution

Use the number on your card or official statement. Ask about holds, recalls, and credential resets.

03 · PRESERVE

Save evidence

Keep messages, receipts, account details, phone numbers, and a timeline of what happened.

04 · REPORT

Use the right channel

Consumer fraud: FTC. Online crime: FBI IC3. Identity theft: IdentityTheft.gov.
